# Evidence-based Decision Boundaries

These rules were calibrated against accepted formal `exam.questions` records for medical-technologist papers. They summarize patterns; they do not copy official questions and do not authorize classification by question number alone.

## General findings

- Some composite papers show stable-looking blocks, but content remains authoritative. Use position only to flag an outlier for human review.
- Classify what the item requires to answer correctly. A term appearing only in a distractor does not create a second label.
- A method can be classified either by its target or its principle. Use the question's requested knowledge to decide.
- When two domains are independently required, retain both selected codes and let a human decide whether to mark `is_comprehensive`.

## 臨床血液學與血庫學

- Red-cell morphology, anemia, hemoglobin and hemolysis are distinct from transfusion immunohematology even when both mention red-cell antibodies.
- DAT in autoimmune hemolysis usually belongs to red-cell hemolysis; DAT in antibody identification or transfusion-reaction investigation belongs to blood bank.
- Platelet disorders, coagulation factors, bleeding, thrombosis and coagulation testing share the hemostasis domain.
- Leukemoid reaction versus leukemia, marrow morphology and hematologic tumor markers belong to white-cell chapters.

## 臨床血清免疫學與臨床病毒學

- General T/B-cell biology, complement, autoantibodies, immunodeficiency and bacterial serology belong to serum immunology.
- A named virus's properties, disease, culture, antiviral treatment or virus-specific result interpretation belong to clinical virology.
- Hemagglutination or ELISA does not decide the domain by itself. Classify the target knowledge, not the laboratory technique word.

## 臨床生理學與病理學

- ECG, pulmonary-function, blood-gas, EEG, nerve-conduction and other function/waveform interpretation belong to clinical physiology.
- Cell injury, inflammation, tissue morphology, organ lesions, neoplasia and histopathology methods belong to pathology.
- The same disease may appear in both domains: an ECG manifestation is physiology; its tissue necrosis is pathology.

## 醫學分子檢驗學與臨床鏡檢學（包括寄生蟲學）

- General microscopy, urine, sputum, CSF, semen, synovial and serous-fluid processing belong to clinical microscopy.
- Parasite morphology, life cycle, host, vectors and organism-centered diagnosis belong to parasitology.
- Genetics, PCR, probes, sequencing, HLA molecular methods and molecular quality control belong to molecular diagnostics.
- For parasite PCR, organism identification belongs to parasitology; primer, amplification or QC principles belong to molecular diagnostics.

## 生物化學與臨床生化學

- Amino-acid properties, protein structural levels and enzyme mechanism belong to basic protein/enzyme.
- Carbohydrate, lipid or nucleic-acid structure and normal pathway chemistry belong to the matching basic chapter.
- Instrument components, specimen analysis, disease markers, hormones, trace elements, organ-function interpretation, therapeutic drug monitoring and analytical quality belong to clinical biochemistry.
- Protein mass spectrometry belongs to clinical biochemistry when the item asks about the instrument or analytical workflow; it belongs to basic protein only when protein chemistry itself is tested.
- Normal lipoprotein composition and transport can belong to basic lipid. Dyslipidemia, risk markers, patient interpretation and measurement belong to clinical biochemistry.
- Glycoprotein bond/structure questions may use protein/enzyme as primary and carbohydrate as secondary when both concepts are independently tested.
